# 投诉模板

把方括号内容替换后提交。保留 App Store 链接、截图、版本号、下载时间。

## Apple 版权投诉

主题：Copyright complaint for [App Name] (App ID [number])

> I am the copyright owner of the source code and original UI assets in [App Name]. The app at [App Store URL] uses my copyrighted code/assets without authorization. The original work is at [GitHub URL], licensed under GPLv3. The reported app [does not provide source / removes copyright notices / uses my app icon and screenshots]. I request removal of the infringing app. Contact: [name, email].

## 商标 / 冒充投诉

主题：Trademark and impersonation complaint for [App Name]

> The reported app uses the "橘阅" name, icon and/or screenshots without permission, and presents itself as the official app. I am the owner of the "橘阅" brand. The official app/project is [URL]. This is likely to confuse users. I request removal or requiring the developer to rebrand. Contact: [name, email].

## GPL 合规通知

主题：GPLv3 compliance notice for [Project]

> You are distributing a modified version of [Project], which is licensed under GNU GPLv3. Your distribution [does not include the license / does not offer corresponding source / removes copyright notices]. GPLv3 requires you to provide the complete corresponding source and preserve notices. Please remedy within [14] days or stop distribution. This is not a waiver of any rights.

## 证据清单

- 原始仓库提交哈希与 LICENSE；
- 对方 App Store 页面、Bundle ID、版本号；
- 代码/资源对比截图；
- 下载与发现时间；
- 对方是否提供源码、是否保留版权声明。
